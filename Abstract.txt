Abstract

Traffic congestion is a major challenge in modern transportation systems, causing increased travel time, fuel consumption, and inconvenience for travelers. Conventional navigation systems generally depend on current traffic conditions to recommend alternative routes, which may result in frequent route changes even when the alternative route provides only a small improvement. To address this problem, this research proposes a Predictive Intelligent Route Recommendation System Using Traffic Forecasting and Adaptive Rerouting Decision. The proposed system combines short-term traffic prediction, route optimization, and an intelligent rerouting decision mechanism into a unified framework.

Historical traffic data from a public dataset such as METR-LA is first preprocessed by handling missing values, removing inconsistencies, and normalizing traffic measurements. A Spatio-Temporal Graph Convolutional Network (STGCN) is then employed to learn both temporal traffic patterns and spatial relationships between connected road segments and predict traffic conditions for the next 15–30 minutes. The predicted traffic speed is converted into estimated travel time for individual road segments. These predicted travel times are assigned as dynamic weights to a road network constructed using OpenStreetMap data. The A* path-finding algorithm is subsequently used to generate the most efficient candidate routes between the source and destination.

The main contribution of the proposed system is an intelligent adaptive rerouting decision engine. Instead of automatically recommending a new route whenever another route becomes slightly faster, the decision engine compares the predicted travel time of the user's current route with alternative routes and calculates the expected time-saving benefit. A rerouting recommendation is generated only when the predicted improvement exceeds a predefined threshold. This approach aims to reduce unnecessary route changes while maintaining efficient travel times. The proposed framework can be evaluated using traffic prediction metrics such as MAE, RMSE, and MAPE, together with route-level measures including travel-time reduction, rerouting frequency, and unnecessary rerouting rate. The system demonstrates how predictive traffic information can be integrated with route optimization and decision-making to provide more stable, efficient, and intelligent route recommendations for dynamic transportation environments.
